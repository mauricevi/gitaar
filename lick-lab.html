Lick Lab

? Record a lick once, then play it with the same fingering over every chord. 1 · Set up first: the chord you play the lick over, the position (where your index finger sits) and a slow tempo; grid and length are in the side panel. Press Record: one bar of clicks, then play. Notes in the count-in bar become pickups. 2 · Check the fingering. Every note goes on the string that fits your position best. Click a note and press Other string to move it; it stays there. Notes can also move earlier or later, be deleted, or be added by clicking the neck. String ↓ / ↑ moves the whole shape one string lower or higher; Octave ↓ / ↑ moves it 12 frets (on another chord only where you play it over that chord). 3 · Pick a chord. The whole shape slides along the neck, so your fingers do exactly the same thing. Auto uses Pat Martino’s minor conversion: a minor lick over a 7 chord starts from its 5 (Am over D7 = D9), over maj7 from its 6 (Am over Cmaj7), over m7♭5 from its ♭3 (Dm over Bm7♭5). Play from picks any other degree. 4 · Practise over the progression with guitar chords, bass and click. In the side panel you can add a few BPM per round. First time? Press Calibrate timing (side panel) and play a short muted note on each of the last eight clicks, so your notes land on the grid.
